Research Profile
Where transfer learning, explainability, and health data intersect.

Machine learning researcher with hands-on experience building and rigorously evaluating deep learning systems for medical and biomedical applications. My core strength lies in transfer-learning-based model development — CNNs, EfficientNet, ResNet, Vision Transformers — paired with explainable AI (Grad-CAM) for auditing model decision-making.

I engineer reproducible, version-controlled research pipelines for large-scale data preprocessing, training, and cross-dataset evaluation. My recent work extends this foundation into large language models for healthcare — fine-tuning Qwen2.5-1.5B with QLoRA on the PubMedQA biomedical question-answering dataset — reflecting a growing interest in applying foundation models to structured clinical and health-related data.

I'm motivated to pursue graduate research in health data science: using large-scale health data and modern AI methods — LLMs, foundation models — for clinical prediction.
